# R9: decision log

| Decision | Options | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Specification: 9 cylinders, 150 x 170 mm, 6.8:1 | - | Set for the exercise, sized as a period radial of this class |
| Master rod 380 mm | 300 mm or 380 mm | At 300 mm the piston skirts foul at BDC; at 380 mm adjacent pistons clear by 26.3 mm |
| Knuckle pins tuned individually | Equal pins, or 16 tuned values | Equal pins spread TDC height by 2.40 mm; tuning costs 0.25° of fixed timing offset |
| Counterweight cancels rotating mass and the whole primary force | Partial or full primary balance | A single-row radial's primary forces sum to a constant rotating vector |
| 2 mm fin clearance flats on each cylinder | Shorter fins, or local flats | Keeps fin area except where neighbours meet; 4 mm gap |
