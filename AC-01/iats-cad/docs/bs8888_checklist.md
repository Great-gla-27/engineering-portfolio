# AC-01-GA-001: BS 8888 checklist

The drawing is a general arrangement for reference, not a manufacturing drawing, so tolerancing and surface texture do not apply. The items below are the BS 8888 (and BS EN ISO 5457, 7200, 128, 129) requirements that do apply to a GA, with how the sheet meets each one. Checked against `outputs/AC-01-GA-001.pdf`. The first pass failed items 10 and 11 (no centre lines; much of the text under 1.8 mm) and both were fixed before this check. Before the drawing is issued on paper, repeat the check on a printed A2 sheet.

| # | Requirement | Standard | How the sheet meets it | Checked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Preferred sheet size, landscape, with a border frame | BS EN ISO 5457 | A2 (594 x 420 mm), 10 mm frame | Met |
| 2 | Grid reference zones in the border | BS EN ISO 5457 | Numbers 1 to 8 along the top and bottom, letters A to F up both sides | Met |
| 3 | Title block in the bottom right corner, with the mandatory fields | BS EN ISO 7200 | Title, drawing number, revision, date of issue, creator, approver, document type, sheet number; owner given by the creator line | Met |
| 4 | Projection method stated and its symbol shown | BS 8888, BS ISO 128-30 | "First angle" in the title block, with the first-angle symbol | Met |
| 5 | Views arranged for the stated projection | BS ISO 128-30 | Plan below the elevation, front view (seen from ahead) to the right of the elevation | Met |
| 6 | Scale stated | BS EN ISO 5455 | 1:100 on A2, in the title block | Met |
| 7 | Units stated, "do not scale" | BS 8888 | "Dimensions in mm. Do not scale." | Met |
| 8 | Dimensions in mm without the unit symbol, closed filled arrowheads, extension lines with a small gap | BS ISO 129-1 | All linear dimensions | Met |
| 9 | Each feature dimensioned once, in the view where it shows best | BS ISO 129-1 | Span and tailplane span in plan; length, height and wheelbase in the elevation; disc diameter, propeller spacing and track in the front view | Met |
| 10 | Line types: continuous for visible outlines, chain for centre and ground lines | BS ISO 128-20 | Visible-edge views from the model; long-dash dot centre lines on the plan and front views (planes of symmetry) and on the ground line | Met (fixed in second pass) |
| 11 | Text upright and legible, minimum 1.8 mm capital height | BS EN ISO 3098 | No text below 7.3 pt (1.8 mm capital height); view titles 8.5 pt | Met (fixed in second pass) |
| 12 | Revision identified | BS 8888 | "A (first issue)" | Met |
| 13 | Source of every value traceable | (project rule) | Values and sources block, plus the master parameter table | Met |
| 14 | Attribution statement | (project rule) | Bottom of the title block | Met |
