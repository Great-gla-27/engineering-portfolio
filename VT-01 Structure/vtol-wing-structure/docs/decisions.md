# VT-01 Structure: decision log

| Decision | Options | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| 16/14 mm CFRP main spar at 25 % chord | 12/10, 14/12, 16/14, 20/18 mm | Sizing estimate (main spar carrying the whole elliptic moment): 285 MPa at ultimate against 540 MPa for 12/10, for 20 g/m more; the 20/18 does not fit the 19.5 mm tip section |
| 6/4 mm rear spar at 62 % chord, held straight | Constant chord fraction, or straight | A spar at a fixed fraction of a tapered chord is not straight; it ends at 56 % chord at the boom rib |
| 3 mm ribs at 100 mm pitch with one lightening hole each | Pitch and hole size | Loads from the span loading, hole sized to the local depth |
| CFRP isotropic at E = 100 GPa in the beam model and in Fusion | Isotropic, or laminate properties | Same material in both, so the comparison tests the method |
| Write the beam prediction before solving the FEA | Predict first, or solve first | A prediction made after the answer is not a check |
